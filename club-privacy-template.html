Data Privacy Notice for League Fixture & Club Night Players

Issued by [Your Club Name] • Season [2026/2027]

1. Introduction & Data Controller

[Your Club Name] ("the Club", "we", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. When you represent our club in league fixtures, cup competitions, or attend our social club night sessions, we collect and process limited personal information to organize match fixtures, schedule team squads, and manage fair court rotations.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR), [Your Club Name] is the Data Controller responsible for your personal data.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information strictly required to run competitive teams and social nights:

  • Identity Details: Your full name and competition division / grading band.
  • Contact Information: Your mobile phone number (for team captain WhatsApp groups) and/or email address.
  • Match Availability: Dates you have marked as available, unavailable, or preferred for upcoming fixtures.

3. Why We Collect It & Lawful Basis

Under GDPR Article 6, we process your information on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of Club Membership & Legitimate Interests: To select squads, prevent team walkovers, avoid double-booking courts, communicate match times, and ensure fair court queues during social club nights.
  • Consent: When you voluntarily submit your upcoming match unavailabilities or substitute preferences via the Player Portal.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored & How It Is Protected

Your data is stored securely within [Your Club Name]'s official, access-controlled Google Workspace (Google Drive & Google Sheets) account.

To automate date matching and avoid hundreds of manual WhatsApp messages, we utilize FixtureFlow software running directly within our club's Google Sheets environment.

  • Zero External Databases: FixtureFlow does not store, harvest, or copy your personal information into any external central database. All fixture rosters and contact entries remain strictly inside our club's Google Workspace custody.
  • Encrypted Connections: Any interaction with our club's self-serve portals (e.g. submitting your availability) occurs over end-to-end encrypted HTTPS connections directly to our club script.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We never sell, rent, or commercialize your personal data. Sharing is strictly limited to fixture management:

  • Team Captains: To see who is available to play on specific fixture dates.
  • Opposition Match Secretaries: Only designated Team Captains' contact details are shared with opponent clubs for match night coordination. Individual player contact numbers and general availability records are never shared with opposing clubs.

6. Retention & Your "Right to Be Forgotten"

We retain player availability data only for the duration of the active sporting season ([e.g. September to May]). At the end of the season, historical rosters are archived or cleared in accordance with club policy.

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the availability and contact data we hold for you.
  • Rectification: Correct any incorrect contact details or match availability.
  • Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request that your contact details and player record be removed from our team spreadsheets at any time by notifying the Match Secretary.

7. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions about this notice, want to update your details, or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact our volunteer match coordination team:

Name: [Secretary Name]
Email: [matchsecretary@yourclub.com]
Phone / WhatsApp: [Contact Number]
