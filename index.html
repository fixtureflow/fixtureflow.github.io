Designed to Eliminate the Chaos

Built by a Match Secretary to solve real-world administrative bottlenecks, making scheduling effortless for everyone.

Zero Back-and-Forth Chaos

Early-season scheduling is traditionally a second job of phone tag and endless emails. FixtureFlow completely automates the initial coordination phase, virtually coordinating match schedules on autopilot so you don't have to lift a finger.

Visual Team Command

Captains get a clean, visual overview of upcoming matches, real-time player availability, and a clear list of eligible substitutes. No more last-minute panic-texting players to fill court spots.

One-Click Availability

Players get a simple, personal portal to block out vacation dates and matches in seconds. The system syncs their schedule instantly, keeping captains and match secretaries aligned all season long.

The Portal Journey

Explore how FixtureFlow coordinates the entire DDLC scheduling experience seamlessly across every user role.

Opponent Match Sec Portal

Eliminate the mismatch of 'what you have' vs. 'what they have'. The Opponent Portal gives external match secretaries a secure, unified view of fixtures, court availability, and scheduling options so they can completely self-serve the final times.

  • Unified source of truth – both clubs see the exact same match data, status, and live court availability, completely eliminating spreadsheet confusion and calendar mistakes.
  • Hands-off initial scheduling – once your preliminary structure is in place, opponents self-serve their times directly from your free slots, completing the schedule without you lifting a finger.
  • Opponent reschedule triggers – opponents get pre-configured messaging triggers to request changes instantly, keeping all requests structured, prefilled, and easy to manage.

Team Independence for Captains

Empower captains to manage their own rosters and reschedules. Instead of texting you every update, captains get a dedicated dashboard to run their teams independently.

  • Roster calendar views – a consolidated monthly matrix showing exactly who is available for upcoming matches.
  • Prefilled reschedule triggers – captains can coordinate reschedules with prefilled, prefilled message scripts ready to send.
  • Smart substitute sourcing – view cross-team availability data to make informed roster adjustments when players are missing.

Effortless Player Control

Take the administrative burden off captains and secretaries. Players get a simple, mobile-first interface to log and manage their own unavailability throughout the season.

  • Mobile-first blocking – a highly visual calendar interface where players can tap to block vacation or busy dates in seconds.
  • Automatic conflict flagging – if a player blocks a date that overlaps with an upcoming draft fixture, the system alerts the captain.
  • Consolidated match calendar – players see all scheduled matches, home/away details, and opponent info in one clear seasonal timeline.
