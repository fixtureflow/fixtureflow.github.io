Privacy Policy

How FixtureFlow protects user privacy through a decentralized, sheet-as-a-database architecture.

Last Updated: September 2026
Are you a Match Secretary or Club Committee Member looking for player notices? View Club Member Template →

1. Introduction

FixtureFlow ("we", "us", or "our") provides software solutions for racket sports clubs, specifically FixtureFlow Leagues (match coordination and fixture management) and CourtFlow (social club night court rotation).

We believe privacy is a foundational engineering requirement, not an afterthought. Unlike conventional SaaS platforms that upload your club's roster, player contact numbers, and member directories to a shared multi-tenant cloud database, FixtureFlow is engineered from the ground up on a decentralized model.

🛡️ Key Architectural Commitment: Zero External Database Storage FixtureFlow does not possess, ingest, harvest, or store your club members' personal data (names, emails, phone numbers, WhatsApp links) in any external or centralized database. All club data remains 100% within your club's authorized Google Workspace account.

2. Data Roles Under GDPR (Controller vs. Processor)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR):

  • The Subscribing Sports Club is the Data Controller: Your club decides which players to roster, collects their contact information and availability, and retains sole custody over the underlying Google Spreadsheet.
  • FixtureFlow is the Software Provider: We provide open-source and proprietary software code that executes directly within the club's Google Workspace environment (via Google Apps Script) and client-side web applications (PWAs hosted via GitHub Pages).

3. What Information Is Handled and Where It Resides

A. Club Fixture & Member Data (Rosters, Availability, Contacts)

  • Storage Location: Solely inside the Google Spreadsheet located in the subscribing club's Google Drive.
  • Processing Mechanism: When a captain, player, or opponent interacts with a FixtureFlow Progressive Web App (PWA), the app communicates directly and encrypted via HTTPS with the club's private Google Apps Script Web App endpoint.
  • Access Controls: Access is gated via 4-digit PIN authentication or cryptographically signed session tokens.

B. Client-Side Browser Storage (`localStorage`)

To ensure smooth offline functionality, prevent session dropouts, and support fast PWA launches, our web applications store minimal operational tokens locally on your device:

  • Session Indicators: Authenticated role tokens (e.g. ff_club_session_token, ff_captain_pin, ff_player_pin) so volunteers do not have to re-enter credentials on every page refresh.
  • Cache Snapshots: Ephemeral UI cache snapshots (e.g. current fixture lists) to enable instant rendering without waiting for cold starts.
  • No Tracking: Browser storage is strictly used for functional application state. We do not use third-party tracking cookies, fingerprinting libraries, or cross-site tracking beacons.

C. Website Visitors (Marketing & Public Pages)

Our public website and static PWA assets are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit fixtureflow.github.io, standard server request headers (such as IP address, user-agent, and request timestamp) may be logged by GitHub in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement for the sole purpose of security, spam prevention, and operational integrity. We do not inject commercial advertising trackers or third-party analytics pixels.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal information under any circumstances. The following infrastructure providers are utilized solely to serve application code:

  • Google Workspace / Google Cloud Platform: Provides the runtime environment (Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Gmail integration) chosen and authorized by the participating club.
  • GitHub Pages: Serves the static, inlined, client-side web application bundles over secure HTTPS.

5. Data Retention & The "Right to Be Forgotten"

Because FixtureFlow does not duplicate or store your data on external servers, exercising your rights under GDPR (such as the Right to Access, Rectification, or Erasure) is immediate and self-contained:

  • Instant Deletion: If a player requests removal under GDPR Article 17 ("Right to Be Forgotten"), the Club Match Secretary simply deletes the corresponding row or player column from their club's Google Sheet. Once cleared from the sheet and flushed from the cache, the data is completely erased from all portals.
  • Season Archiving: At the conclusion of a sporting season, clubs have complete autonomy to archive or wipe their Google Sheets according to their own internal club retention policies.

6. Security Safeguards

Our software enforces industry-standard technical safeguards:

  • End-to-End HTTPS: All network traffic between client browsers, GitHub Pages, and Google Apps Script is encrypted with TLS 1.3.
  • Brute-Force Rate Limiting: Authentication endpoints enforce server-side execution delays on consecutive failed PIN attempts to resist brute-force enumeration.
  • Input Sanitization: Public parameters and host deployment IDs are strictly validated via hardened regular expressions to prevent injection, open redirects, or unauthorized host spoofing.
  • Audit Logging: System activities, reschedules, and booking transactions are logged locally into dedicated spreadsheet audit tabs (`Event_Log`) visible exclusively to authorized club administrators.
